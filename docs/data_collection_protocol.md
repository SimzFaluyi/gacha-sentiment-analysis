# Proposed Data Collection Protocol

## Platform

Reddit

## Games

- Genshin Impact
- Honkai: Star Rail
- Zenless Zone Zero

## Observation Period

1 January 2025 – 31 December 2025

## Target Sample

Approximately 4,000–6,000 relevant comments.

The final number would depend on the availability of appropriately
authorised data.

---

### Gacha Mechanics

### Monetisation

- monetisation
- monetization

---

## Inclusion Criteria

A record would be included if it:

- originates from an approved target community;
- falls within the specified date range;
- is primarily English-language;
- discusses or directly relates to monetisation; and
- contains sufficient text for meaningful sentiment analysis.

---

## Exclusion Criteria

A record would be excluded if it is:

- duplicated;
- bot-generated;
- deleted;
- non-English;
- an irrelevant keyword match; or
- too short to provide meaningful sentiment information.

---

## Proposed Data Fields

The minimum required research fields would be documented before
approved collection.

Potential fields include:

- anonymised record identifier;
- community;
- game;
- timestamp;
- text;
- parent/post relationship where required;
- matched sampling term.

Unnecessary personal information would not be collected.

---

## Current Status

The proposed collection was not conducted because the required
institutional ethical approval had not been obtained.

This repository therefore contains no empirical Reddit dataset.
