# Sentiment Annotation Guidelines

## Purpose

The proposed annotation process is designed to label sentiment towards
gacha-game monetisation systems.

The annotation target is not the player's overall opinion of the game.

---

## Labels

### Positive

Use Positive when the comment expresses a favourable attitude towards
the monetisation issue.

Examples:

- "The pity system makes pulling much less stressful."
- "I think the guaranteed system is fair."

---

### Negative

Use Negative when the comment expresses an unfavourable attitude
towards the monetisation issue.

Examples:

- "The banner rates are awful."
- "The pulls are far too expensive."

---

### Neutral

Use Neutral when the comment is primarily factual, descriptive,
ambiguous, or does not express a clearly positive or negative
attitude towards monetisation.

Examples:

- "The next banner starts next week."
- "I have 80 wishes saved."

---

## Mixed Sentiment

When a comment contains both positive and negative opinions, the
annotation should focus on the overall sentiment towards the
monetisation issue.

The final annotation rule should be established before empirical
labelling begins.

---

## Sarcasm

Sarcasm should be identified where possible from the context available
within the comment.

Ambiguous cases should be flagged for review rather than assigned
arbitrarily.

---

## Irrelevant Content

Comments that contain a monetisation keyword but do not actually
discuss monetisation should be excluded.

---

## Inter-Rater Agreement

If a second coder is available, a subset of comments should be
independently labelled.

Agreement should be assessed using an appropriate inter-rater
agreement statistic.

---

## Important Limitation

These guidelines describe the proposed annotation protocol.

Final empirical annotation was not completed because the planned Reddit
dataset was not collected.
