# Methodology

## Research Design

The proposed research uses a mixed-methods methodology combining
quantitative sentiment classification with qualitative thematic analysis.

The quantitative component compares three NLP approaches:

1. VADER
2. TF-IDF + Logistic Regression
3. DistilBERT

The qualitative component uses thematic analysis to identify recurring
monetisation-related themes.

---

## Research Questions

### RQ1

What sentiments are expressed in discussions relating to monetisation
systems in selected gacha games?

### RQ2

How effectively can different NLP approaches classify player sentiment
towards these monetisation systems?

---

## Scope

The proposed study focuses on:

- Genshin Impact
- Honkai: Star Rail
- Zenless Zone Zero

The proposed observation period is:

1 January 2025 – 31 December 2025.

---

## Sampling

The proposed data source is Reddit.

Criterion-based sampling would use predefined monetisation-related
keywords.

The proposed target is approximately 4,000–6,000 relevant comments,
subject to availability through an authorised data-access route and
institutional ethical approval.

---

## Sentiment Categories

Three sentiment categories are proposed:

- Positive
- Neutral
- Negative

Sentiment refers specifically to the player's attitude towards the
monetisation issue under discussion.

---

## Evaluation

The proposed quantitative evaluation uses:

- precision;
- recall;
- macro-F1;
- confusion matrices; and
- class-distribution analysis.

Macro-F1 is emphasised because class imbalance may occur.

---

## Current Research Status

The empirical Reddit dataset was not collected because the required
institutional ethical approval had not been obtained.

Consequently, no empirical player-level conclusions are reported.
