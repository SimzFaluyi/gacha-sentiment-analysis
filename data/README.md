# Data

## Research Dataset

No Reddit research data are included in this repository.

The proposed empirical study was designed to analyse Reddit discussions
relating to:

- Genshin Impact
- Honkai: Star Rail
- Zenless Zone Zero

during the period:

**1 January 2025 – 31 December 2025**

However, the planned Reddit data collection was not conducted because
the required institutional ethical approval had not been obtained.

---

## Sample Data

The `sample_data.csv` file contains synthetic demonstration data.

It is provided only to demonstrate and test the computational pipeline.

The sample data:

- are not collected from Reddit;
- do not represent actual Reddit users;
- do not constitute the research dataset; and
- must not be interpreted as empirical findings.

---

## Data Protection

No usernames, Reddit IDs, personal information, credentials or
restricted research data should be added to this repository.
